# Rough category benchmarks (share of monthly spending)

These are common rules of thumb for planning, not strict targets. Individual situations differ.

## Housing and utilities
Rent or housing is commonly kept to around 25-35% of take-home income. Utilities such as electricity, water, internet and phone usually add roughly 5-10% together.

## Groceries and food
Groceries are often around 10-15% of spending. Eating out and food delivery combined are often kept under about 5-10%. If food delivery is larger than groceries, that is usually a sign to review it.

## Transport
Transport (fuel, cabs, public transport) is commonly around 5-10% of spending for people who commute. A share well above that is worth checking against cheaper options.

## Shopping and entertainment
Shopping, entertainment and subscriptions together are wants. A common guide is to keep them within about 10-15% of spending, with subscriptions alone under roughly 2-3%.

## Health and insurance
Health and insurance are protective spending and should not be cut to meet a budget target. Compare plans when renewing, but keep adequate cover.

## Miscellaneous
When the Miscellaneous category is large, it is usually a sign of many untracked small payments. A healthy habit is to keep it small by naming where small payments go.
